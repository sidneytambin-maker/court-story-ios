# Tennis Sounds

Five independently recorded tennis sounds, with CC0 public-domain dedications
verified on their individual source pages on 10 September 2026. No paid clips,
non-commercial licences, repeated bounce variants or synthesized substitutes.

| Choice | Creator | Recording and licence page |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Racket strike | jacklilley | https://freesound.org/people/jacklilley/sounds/338122/ |
| Racket swoosh | MIKEJONESBONES | https://freesound.org/people/MIKEJONESBONES/sounds/511825/ |
| New ball can | tomschuetz | https://freesound.org/people/tomschuetz/sounds/649763/ |

CC0 dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
LaSonotheque licence statement: https://lasonotheque.org/licences.html
CC0 permits free commercial use, editing and redistribution in the application.
Courtesy credits are also accessible from Notifications & Sounds in Settings.

The downloaded source files are retained here. Freesound files are its publicly
linked high-quality MP3 renditions. No account-gated downloads were accessed.
The applause recording is from a tennis court; the other recordings document
ball bouncing, racket impact, a tennis-racket swing and a pressurised tennis can.

`Scripts/prepare_tennis_sounds.py` takes one excerpt from each independent source,
converts to mono 16-bit PCM WAV at 48 kHz, reduces peaks and fades edges. Outputs
last 0.28 to 1.2 seconds. No source is reused for a second choice. The precise
download URLs, source hashes, trim points and output hashes are in manifest.json.
