Court Story connection invitation

Connect with a player or coach. Opening this page does not share any sporting records.

Checking the invitation format…

Keep your invitation code

If the app did not open, copy this code. Open Court Story, go to Settings, then Court Story Connect, sign in and choose Review invitation.

Installing Court Story

Use the testing invitation supplied by Court Story to install the app. Then return to this page, copy the code and enter it in Court Story Connect. Store installation does not automatically carry this code into the app.

Your approval stays in your control

The app checks whether the invitation has expired, has already been used or was addressed to another account. Invitations expire after 48 hours. The player must approve the coach and choose what to share before access begins.

Health data and media are excluded. You can manage sharing and notifications in Court Story Connect at any time.
