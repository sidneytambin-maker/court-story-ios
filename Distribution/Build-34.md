# Court Story Build 34

Version 0.1.0 (34). Candidate in validation, not yet uploaded or available to testers.

## What To Test

- Native Coach and Player welcome on iPhone and Apple Watch, multiple sports and independent athlete preferences.
- Official scoring formats for ten racket sports, plus named custom numeric sports. Named doubles service, receivers, courts, correction and undo follow the selected sport.
- Coach roster, observations, practice plans, measured drills, player progress by period and private progress-report previews.
- Photos and short video clips on iPhone, accessible moments and honest linked context on Watch. Full private library packages include originals; legacy JSON does not.
- Basic, Standard and Power keep their appropriate controls while retaining existing richer records when changing mode.
- Twenty-five achievements, sport-scoped dashboards and complications, and notifications that open the correct record and purpose.
- Watch Health authorization waits for Apple's permission-database handoff before checking workout-write status. Simulator tests cover a real HealthKit session, save and independent readback.

## Release Gates

Native iPhone and Watch tests, screenshot and accessibility-tree review, fresh-install privacy checks and unsigned archive inspection must pass for the exact candidate. Signing credentials remain local. The signed IPA must include the correct Watch app and widget before Apple upload.

Availability is confirmed only after Apple reports valid processing, approved beta review and testing availability in the existing groups. Build 33 remains the last verified live beta until then.

Physical wrist sensor readings, paired-device transport and actual VoiceOver listening are device-test requirements, not simulator passes. No personal library, coaching notes, media or signing credentials are included in the public source or tester app.
